# Qissora — Kids Islamic Audio Stories

An audio story app for children. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and
nothing about the child ever leaves the device.

The stories stream from our own host and are kept on the device after the
first play. A parent can sign in with Google to buy or restore Qissora
Premium; that is the only part of the app that involves an account, and it is
optional.
