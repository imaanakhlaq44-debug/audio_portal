## adam_en — Hazrat Adam (A.S.)

### Ep 1 · Before the First Footstep
title: Leave a Good Footprint
mission: Today, do one good act without being asked and without announcing it afterward. Help someone, give another person a turn, pick up something someone dropped, assist a younger child, care for an animal, or make life easier for someone around you.
levelUp: Do three quiet good acts in three different places - one at home, one at school, and one with a friend or neighbour.

### Ep 2 · The Secret of Knowledge
title: Use What You Know to Help
mission: Today, use one thing you are good at to help someone who finds it difficult. Teach without showing off and without making the other person feel small.
levelUp: Help someone learn the skill without taking over the task. Let them try it themselves.

### Ep 3 · I Am Better
title: Lower the Crown
mission: Find one moment today when you could put yourself first and deliberately choose respect instead. Give someone a turn, congratulate someone who outperforms you, listen without interrupting, include someone overlooked, or apologise first when you know you were wrong.
levelUp: Choose one person you often compare yourself with and do one sincere good act for that person.

### Ep 4 · The Forbidden Tree
title: Say No to One Wrong Choice
mission: Today, when someone invites you to do something you know is wrong, refuse calmly. Do not copy, tease, hide someone's belongings, lie for a friend, break a queue, open something without permission, or join harmful behaviour simply because others are doing it.
levelUp: If a friend is being pressured into something wrong, stand beside them and make it easier for them to say no too.

### Ep 5 · The Door Was Still Open
title: Own It and Repair It
mission: Correct one mistake you have made instead of hiding it or blaming someone else. Admit what you did, apologise sincerely, and make an effort to repair the harm. Do not add 'but...' after your apology.
levelUp: If the other person forgives you, change the behaviour that caused the problem instead of assuming the job is finished.

### Ep 6 · The Fire Called Envy
title: Celebrate, Don't Compete
mission: Today, when someone receives something good that you also wanted - a good mark, praise, a turn, a role, a prize or attention - do one sincere act of kindness toward them. Congratulate them, encourage them, help them, or celebrate their success. Do not add a comparison about yourself afterward.
levelUp: Choose someone you regularly compete with and make your sincere act of kindness toward that person specifically.

## fairness_en — The Golden Balance

### Ep 1 · The Golden Balance
title: Fairness Without a Prize
mission: Do one fair thing today when nobody is rewarding you for it. Give someone a turn, invite a quiet child into a game, share fairly, or let another person speak before you. Ask yourself: Would I still do this if nobody noticed?
levelUp: Notice one moment when fairness costs you something small, such as giving up the first turn, and choose fairness anyway.

### Ep 2 · A Shadow in the Photograph
title: Ask Before You Blame
mission: When something goes wrong today, do not accuse anyone on a guess. First separate what you know from what you only suspect. Use the sentence: 'Let us find out what happened before we blame anyone.'
levelUp: Write one fact you know and one thing you were only guessing.

### Ep 3 · The Bronze Key
title: Listen to Both Sides
mission: Choose a harmless decision with friends or siblings. Let each person explain their choice without interruption. Then suggest a fair solution rather than automatically choosing your closest friend's idea.
levelUp: Repeat each person's reason fairly before giving your own opinion.

### Ep 4 · The Garden of Balance
title: Everyone Gets a Turn
mission: During one group activity, make sure everyone gets one turn before anyone gets a second turn. Politely invite somebody who has not had a chance yet.
levelUp: Notice whether the loudest person is receiving more opportunities than the quietest person. Help balance the turns respectfully.

### Ep 5 · The Fair Path
title: Fair Does Not Always Mean Same
mission: Help two people in the way each person actually needs. One person may need more time, another an explanation, and another encouragement. Fairness can require different support for a fair chance.
levelUp: Think of one example where giving exactly the same help to everyone would not actually be fair.

### Ep 6 · The Missing Balance
title: Put the Brakes on a Rumour
mission: If you hear an unconfirmed story about another child, do not pass it on. Say kindly, 'Maybe we should not blame anyone until we know what happened.'
levelUp: Explain the difference between a fact, a guess and a rumour.

### Ep 7 · The Number Seventeen
title: Family Fairness Meeting
mission: Choose one small family matter - turns, a shared chore, choosing a programme, using a device, or sharing a treat. Give each person a chance to speak and agree on one fair rule for the day.
levelUp: Ask whether the fairest solution must always give everyone exactly the same thing.

### Ep 8 · The Hidden Passage
title: Fact or Guess Detective
mission: Use an imaginary mystery such as a missing book or changed game rule. List what you KNOW and what you are only GUESSING. Do not accuse a real person.
levelUp: Ask what evidence would be needed before reaching a fair conclusion.

### Ep 9 · The Record of Contestant Seventeen
title: Make It Right
mission: Think of one small moment when you may have been unfair - interrupting someone, taking an extra turn, excluding someone or judging too quickly. If appropriate, apologise and take one action to repair the harm.
levelUp: Use three steps: 'I was wrong.' 'I am sorry.' 'How can I make it right?'

### Ep 10 · Amanpur's Promise
title: My Golden Balance Promise
mission: Write or draw one fairness promise for the next seven days, such as 'I will listen before judging,' 'I will give others a fair turn,' or 'I will not spread rumours.' Share it with someone who can encourage you.
levelUp: At the end of the week, write one sentence about the hardest moment when you chose fairness.

## gratitude_en — The Things We Never Notice

### Ep 1 · The List That Wouldn’t End
title: Notice and Lighten One Load
mission: Choose one person whose work you usually benefit from without noticing. Make one small part of their work easier today: put your own things away, carry something, clean up after yourself, return a trolley or chair, or help before being asked.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Do a good act without telling anyone you did it. Let the reduced burden be the thank-you.

### Ep 2 · The Man at the Gate
title: Learn a Name, Show Respect
mission: Learn the name of one person you regularly see but rarely speak to: a guard, cleaner, driver, helper, librarian, shop worker, neighbour or support staff member. Greet them respectfully and make one small act easier for them.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Use their name respectfully the next time you see them and continue the habit for the week.

### Ep 3 · The Teacher in the Photograph
title: Thank a Teacher Through Action
mission: Choose one teacher today and show gratitude through behaviour: arrive prepared, complete the work carefully, help organise the classroom, listen without interrupting, or assist another student so the teacher does not have to repeat the same task.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Do the action before saying thank you. Let your conduct be part of the gratitude.

### Ep 4 · The Missing Page
title: Protect What Others Prepare for You
mission: Take responsibility for one thing someone usually prepares or fixes for you: pack your own school bag, fill your bottle, put away clothes, clean your eating place, organise your shoes, or prepare what you need for the next day.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Keep that responsibility for three days without being reminded.

### Ep 5 · When the House Went Quiet
title: Carry One Family Responsibility
mission: Choose one household responsibility that normally belongs to someone else and carry it properly today: set and clear the table, fold laundry, prepare school items, refill water, help a younger sibling, organise a shared space, or assist an elder.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Do not treat it as a one-time favour. Repeat the same responsibility for the next three days.

### Ep 6 · The Work We Never Saw
title: Do the Job That Is “Not Mine”
mission: When you see a small useful task that safely needs doing, help even if nobody assigned it to you: pick up litter, return equipment, stack chairs, carry books, wipe a shared table, or help restore a space after an activity.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Choose a task that benefits everyone but gives you no personal reward or recognition.

### Ep 7 · The Silver Key
title: Protect a Trust
mission: Return one borrowed item today in the same or better condition than you received it. If you have promised to keep, carry, deliver or care for something, complete that responsibility without needing a reminder.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Choose one trust you have been careless with before and repair that habit this week.

### Ep 8 · The Woman Who Came Back
title: Take the First Good Step
mission: If there is a safe relationship in which you have been rude, distant or stubborn, take one respectful step today: apologise, return something, greet the person, stop an unkind joke, or offer help.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Do not wait for the other person to make the first move if you already know what your own right action should be.

### Ep 9 · While They Can Hear You
title: Thank Them While They Can Hear You
mission: Choose one person who regularly helps, teaches, protects, supports or cares for you. Thank them today with one specific sentence about what they actually do, then follow the words with one helpful action.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Choose someone you have assumed “already knows” you are grateful. Tell them anyway.

### Ep 10 · The Children Who Notice
title: Pass It On
mission: Do one act of kindness for someone who cannot repay you today: help a new student, assist a younger child, support an elderly person, share useful material, clean a shared place, or make someone’s task easier. Do not ask for a reward.
levelUp: (Ages 9-12): Repeat one gratitude action from any earlier challenge and turn it into a weekly habit.

## honesty_en — The Book of Trust

### Ep 1 · The Missing Notebook
title: Tell the Small Truth
mission: Today, tell one small truth that would be easier to hide. Admit a forgotten task, a broken item, an unfinished responsibility, or another mistake without inventing an excuse. Keep the truth simple and complete.
levelUp: After telling the truth, take one action to repair the problem instead of expecting the words alone to fix it.

### Ep 2 · The Mysterious Footprints
title: Stop One Rumour
mission: If you hear an unverified story about someone today, do not repeat it. If another child asks you what happened, say only what you actually know. If you do not know, use the honest sentence: "I do not know."
levelUp: If you have already repeated an unverified story, go back to the person you told and correct it.

### Ep 3 · The Blue Thread
title: Finish the Sentence
mission: Think of one situation where you have told only the safe part of the truth. Correct it today. Give the missing detail calmly and without blaming somebody else.
levelUp: If your hidden detail caused a problem for someone, take one practical step to repair that harm.

### Ep 4 · The Test of Trust
title: Protect an Amanah
mission: Take one item, duty, message, or responsibility that someone has trusted you with today and protect it carefully. Return it on time, complete the duty properly, and do not use it for yourself without permission.
levelUp: Do the trusted task without reminding people afterward how responsible you were.

### Ep 5 · The Missing Money
title: Use "I Do Not Know"
mission: Today, when you are unsure about an answer, event, rumour, or another person's intention, do not guess as though you are certain. Say, "I do not know," or "I am not sure," and stop there.
levelUp: Catch yourself once before adding an unverified detail to a story and remove that detail.

### Ep 6 · The Shadow of Suspicion
title: Check Before You Claim
mission: Before you say that someone did something wrong today, check what you actually saw or heard. If you only have a guess, keep the person's name out of the story.
levelUp: If you realise you judged someone too quickly, correct your words before the day ends.

### Ep 7 · The Phone That Changed Everything
title: Return What Is Not Yours
mission: If you find something that does not belong to you today, do not keep it, hide it, or treat it as a lucky find. Return it to the owner or hand it to a responsible adult.
levelUp: If you borrowed something and have delayed returning it, return it today in the condition you received it - or explain honestly if it was damaged.

### Ep 8 · The Empty Seat
title: Repair One Unfair Judgment
mission: If you have spoken unfairly about someone, laughed at their embarrassment, or repeated a suspicion, correct one thing today. Apologise or tell the people who heard you that you did not have enough evidence.
levelUp: Do one respectful act toward someone who is being left out or judged by others, without making a public show of rescuing them.

### Ep 9 · The Truth on the Screen
title: Hidden Honesty
mission: Do one honest action today that nobody needs to praise you for: return extra money, correct a score, admit you caused a mess, refuse an unfair advantage, or keep a promise when nobody is checking.
levelUp: Do not tell anyone about the action afterward unless telling them is necessary to repair something.

### Ep 10 · When the Truth Was Revealed
title: Rebuild Trust With Action
mission: Choose one relationship where your actions have weakened trust. Do one concrete repair today: apologise without excuses, return what you owe, complete a promise, correct a lie, or show up for a responsibility you have avoided.
levelUp: Repeat the repaired behaviour consistently for the next three opportunities. Trust is rebuilt by patterns, not one dramatic apology.

## hud_en — Hazrat Hud (A.S.)

### Ep 1 · Nobody Can Beat Us
title: Make Your Strength Useful
mission: Choose one thing you are good at. Do not hide it and do not boast about it. Say: “Alhamdulillah, Allah has given me this ability.” Then use that ability to help one person today. At the end, ask yourself: “Did my ability make someone feel smaller, or did it make me more useful?”
levelUp: Watch yourself today for one warning sign of arrogance: laughing at someone’s weakness, refusing advice, expecting to be better than everyone, or looking down on another person. If you notice it, correct yourself immediately. Confidence recognises the gift. Gratitude remembers the Giver. Arrogance begins when the gift makes us forget both.

### Ep 2 · Look What We Built
title: Create Without Comparing
mission: Make or complete something today — a drawing, school assignment, model, piece of writing, organised space or any useful task. Try to make it excellent, but do not compare it with someone else’s work merely to feel superior. When you finish, ask: “Did I make this better because I wanted to do good work, or because I wanted to prove that I am better?”
levelUp: Find one good quality in the work of someone whose achievement is smaller, simpler or different from yours, and genuinely appreciate it. Excellence says, “How can I improve my work?” Arrogance says, “How can I prove my work is better than yours?”

### Ep 3 · Who Gave You All This?
title: Count What You Received
mission: Choose one achievement you are proud of today. It might be a good mark, a sporting ability, something you made, a skill you learned or a responsibility you completed. First say what you contributed: practice, effort, patience, planning or discipline. Then name at least three things you received that helped you: health, parents, teachers, time, knowledge, opportunity, materials or someone’s advice. Finish by saying: “Alhamdulillah for what Allah gave me, and Alhamdulillah that He allowed me to use it.”
levelUp: Thank one person whose contribution to your success is easy to overlook. Arrogance remembers only, “I achieved.” Gratitude also remembers, “I received.”

### Ep 4 · Strong Enough to Be Kind
title: Use Your Strength to Make Someone Stronger
mission: Choose one situation today in which you know more, can do more, are older, stronger or have more authority than someone else. Instead of taking over, help that person learn to do something themselves. Ask: “Am I helping because I am stronger, or controlling because I am stronger?”
levelUp: If you are given leadership today, practise these three actions: listen before deciding, explain instead of ordering when possible, and give someone else a chance to contribute. Arrogance uses strength to dominate. Good character uses strength to protect, teach and serve.

### Ep 5 · When Strength Was Not Enough
title: Turn Pride into Gratitude
mission: Think of one blessing that sometimes makes you feel proud: your intelligence, strength, appearance, possessions, marks, popularity, family, talent or achievements. Say: “Alhamdulillah. Allah gave me this blessing, and I am responsible for how I use it.” Then use that blessing in one way that benefits someone else.
levelUp: When you succeed, practise three steps: thank Allah, appreciate the people who helped you, and ask how you can use the success responsibly. Confidence says, “I can try.” Arrogance says, “I cannot fall.” Humility says, “Whatever strength I have is from Allah.”

## nuh_en — Hazrat Nuh (A.S.)

### Ep 1 · What Was the Point?
title: Do Good Without Waiting for Thanks
mission: Choose one shared place today and make it slightly better. It may be your classroom, home, street, mosque area, playground or park. Put something in its proper place, safely remove rubbish, organise something, or help maintain cleanliness. Do it because it is good, not because you expect someone to notice you. If nobody thanks you, remind yourself: “Allah knows why I did it.”
levelUp: If someone undoes your good work, do not immediately ask, “What was the point?” Instead ask: “What can I do differently so this good effort lasts longer?”

### Ep 2 · Try Again, Differently
title: Try a Better Way
mission: Choose one good thing you have been trying to do but which is not working very well. Instead of giving up, change the method. Ask yourself: “Is the goal wrong, or does my method need improvement?”
levelUp: Choose one useful message today and communicate it in two different respectful ways. Keep the principle the same, but improve how you deliver it.

### Ep 3 · When People Laugh
title: Do Not Let Laughter Change What Is Right
mission: Choose one good, sensible action that you sometimes feel embarrassed doing because other people may laugh or tease you. It might be keeping a place clean, refusing to cheat, speaking respectfully, helping someone, admitting a mistake, praying when appropriate, or refusing to join something wrong. Before stopping because of other people’s reactions, ask yourself: “Would this action still be right if nobody laughed at me?” If the answer is yes, do not let ridicule alone make the decision for you.
levelUp: If someone mocks you today, do not rush to insult them back. Give yourself a few seconds and choose between three responses: ignore it, answer calmly, or seek help from a responsible adult if the behaviour becomes repeated or harmful. Patience does not mean becoming weak. It means refusing to let someone else’s bad behaviour control yours.

### Ep 4 · Only a Few
title: Do Not Measure Goodness Only by Numbers
mission: Choose one good activity today and do it even if only one person joins you, or even if nobody joins you at all. Help clean a shared place, revise with a friend, assist someone younger, organise something useful, or begin a positive habit. Do not ask only, “How many people noticed?” Also ask: “Was the action good, was my intention sincere, and did I do my part properly?”
levelUp: Today, notice one situation where you are tempted to follow something merely because everyone else is doing it. Pause and ask: “Would this still be right if I were the only person choosing it?” A crowd does not turn wrong into right, and a small number does not make truth unimportant.

### Ep 5 · Build Before They Understand
title: Build Something That Can Continue
mission: Instead of doing one good action only once, choose one small way to make that goodness easier to continue. For example, create a simple study timetable instead of depending on motivation, place a waste basket where rubbish usually gathers, organise your books so you can keep your room tidy, make a family reminder for saving water, or create a rota so one person is not responsible for everything. Ask yourself: “What can I build today that makes tomorrow’s good action easier?”
levelUp: Before starting a new idea, check four things: Is the goal good? Is the method sensible? Is it safe? Have I asked the right people for advice or permission? Then begin. Do not abandon a sensible good effort simply because someone says, “That will never work.” But remain humble enough to improve it when the evidence shows that something needs changing.

### Ep 6 · What Remains
title: Leave Something Good Behind
mission: Choose one good action this week that can continue benefiting someone after you have finished doing it. You might teach a younger child a useful habit, organise something so others can use it easily, place a reminder where your family can benefit, help create a system for keeping a shared place clean, teach someone something you know, or begin a habit that another person can continue. Do not make your main question: “Will people remember that I started it?” Instead ask: “Will something good remain because I tried?”
levelUp: At the end of the week, review one effort you made and divide the result into two parts. Ask yourself: What was within my control? My intention, effort, behaviour, preparation, honesty, patience and willingness to improve. Then ask: What was outside my control? Other people’s appreciation, their choices, immediate results and whether everything happened exactly as I hoped. Work on the first part. Entrust the second to Allah.

## patience_en — The Things That Take Time

### Ep 1 · Why Can’t It Happen Now?
title: Do One Part Today
mission: Pick something you want finished right now - a drawing, a model, a chapter, a tidy room - and do only one part of it today, properly. Do not rush towards the end. Measure, check, and correct as you go.

### Ep 2 · The Easy Way
title: Skip the Shortcut
mission: Notice one moment today when you are about to take the easy way - guessing instead of reading, hiding a mistake instead of fixing it, or rushing a job you were asked to do carefully. Choose the slower, proper way instead.
levelUp: If something goes missing or goes wrong, search calmly and check one place at a time instead of blaming someone.

### Ep 3 · Not Everything Has an Answer
title: Wait Without Pushing
mission: There will be something today that you want to know and are not told - a surprise, a grown-up conversation, a decision still being made. Ask once, politely, then let it be. Patience is also respecting a boundary while curiosity is pushing.
levelUp: Notice the restless feeling of wanting to know, and say to yourself: "I can wait, and I will still be alright."

### Ep 4 · The First Step
title: Do the Next Thing
mission: Think of something so big that you keep putting it off. You may not be able to do all of it today, but you can do the next thing. Write down the very next small step, and do only that.

### Ep 5 · Three Days
title: Be Patient With Yourself
mission: Today, when you make a mistake at something you have been practising, do not call yourself careless or slow. Say instead: "I am still learning this." Then try it once more.
levelUp: Ages 9-12: Think of one thing you are hard on yourself about, and give yourself the same kindness you would give a friend who was still learning it.

levelUp: Choose one family rule you do not fully understand. Instead of complaining or secretly breaking it, respectfully ask: “Can you explain what this rule is protecting?” Then listen carefully before deciding what you think.

### Ep 2 · But Tell Me Why
title: Ask to Understand
mission: Choose one family rule that sometimes frustrates you. Ask your parent or guardian respectfully: “Can you help me understand what this rule is protecting?” Listen to the full answer before replying.
levelUp: After the reason is explained, ask yourself: “Am I still asking questions because I need understanding, or because I want the answer to change?” Understanding a boundary gives us knowledge. What we do after understanding it shows our character.

### Ep 3 · That Was My Turn
title: Respect Someone Else’s Share
mission: Today, notice one shared thing: food, screen time, a toy, a seat, space, money, equipment or someone’s time. Before taking more, ask: “Am I using my share, or am I beginning to use someone else’s?”
levelUp: Practise waiting for one turn today without complaining or trying to extend your time. Something may be available in front of you and still belong to someone else’s right.

### Ep 4 · Ammi Will Never Know
title: Do Not Hide Behind the Group
mission: Today, notice one moment when someone says: “Everyone is doing it.” Before joining, ask: “If I were the only person making this choice, would I still think it was right?”
levelUp: Watch for these excuses: “Only once.” “Only a little.” “Nobody will know.” “Everyone agrees.” If you hear yourself using one of them, stop and reconsider. Other people can influence your choice. They cannot carry your responsibility for you.

### Ep 5 · The Rule Was Not the Enemy
title: Find What the Boundary Protects
mission: Choose one reasonable rule at home that sometimes annoys you. Ask yourself: “What person, right, responsibility or future consequence might this rule be protecting?” Try to respect it today without needing someone to remind you.
levelUp: When you disagree with a family boundary, practise three steps: Ask respectfully. Listen carefully. Respond responsibly. A boundary can feel like an obstacle when we only see what it stops. Wisdom begins when we also see what it protects.

## salih_ur — حضرت صالح علیہ السلام

### قسط 1 · ہر چیز کے لیے اصول کیوں ہیں؟
title: کیا میں صرف کر سکتا ہوں، یا مجھے کرنے کا حق بھی ہے؟
mission: آج ایسے ایک موقع کو محسوس کریں جب آپ کوئی کام کرسکتے ہوں، لیکن پہلے یہ سوچنا چاہیے کہ کیا آپ کو وہ کرنے کا حق بھی ہے۔ عمل کرنے سے پہلے خود سے پوچھیں: "صرف اس لیے کہ میں یہ کرسکتا ہوں، کیا اس کا مطلب یہ ہے کہ مجھے یہ کرنا بھی چاہیے؟"
levelUp: گھر کے کسی ایسے اصول کا انتخاب کریں جسے آپ پوری طرح نہیں سمجھتے۔ شکایت کرنے یا چپکے سے اسے توڑنے کے بجائے احترام سے پوچھیں: "کیا آپ سمجھا سکتے ہیں کہ یہ اصول کس چیز کی حفاظت کررہا ہے؟" پھر فیصلہ کرنے سے پہلے جواب کو غور سے سنیں۔ اگلی قسط: لیکن مجھے بتائیں کیوں؟

### قسط 2 · لیکن مجھے بتائیں، کیوں؟
title: سمجھنے کے لیے پوچھیں
mission: گھر کے کسی ایسے اصول کا انتخاب کریں جو کبھی کبھی آپ کو پریشان کرتا ہے۔ اپنے والدین یا سرپرست سے احترام کے ساتھ پوچھیں: "کیا آپ مجھے سمجھا سکتے ہیں کہ یہ اصول کس چیز کی حفاظت کررہا ہے؟" جواب دینے سے پہلے پورا جواب غور سے سنیں۔
levelUp: وجہ سمجھائے جانے کے بعد خود سے پوچھیں: "کیا میں اب بھی اس لیے سوال کررہا ہوں کہ مجھے سمجھنے کی ضرورت ہے، یا اس لیے کہ میں چاہتا ہوں کہ جواب بدل جائے؟" کسی حد کو سمجھنا ہمیں علم دیتا ہے۔ لیکن اسے سمجھنے کے بعد ہم کیا کرتے ہیں، یہی ہمارے کردار کو ظاہر کرتا ہے۔ اگلی قسط: یہ میری باری تھی۔

### قسط 3 · یہ میری باری تھی
title: کسی دوسرے کے حصے کا احترام کریں
mission: آج کسی ایک مشترکہ چیز پر توجہ دیں: کھانا، اسکرین ٹائم، کھلونا، نشست، جگہ، پیسہ، سامان یا کسی کا وقت۔ مزید لینے سے پہلے خود سے پوچھیں: ’’کیا میں اپنا حصہ استعمال کر رہا ہوں، یا اب کسی دوسرے کے حصے کو استعمال کرنا شروع کر رہا ہوں؟‘‘
levelUp: آج ایک باری کا انتظار کرنے کی مشق کریں، بغیر شکایت کیے یا اپنا وقت بڑھانے کی کوشش کیے۔ کوئی چیز آپ کے سامنے موجود ہو سکتی ہے، لیکن پھر بھی وہ کسی دوسرے کے حق میں شامل ہو سکتی ہے۔ اگلی قسط: امی کو کبھی پتا نہیں چلے گا۔

### قسط 4 · امی کو کبھی پتا نہیں چلے گا
title: دوسروں کی آڑ نہ لیں
mission: آج ایک ایسا لمحہ نوٹ کریں جب کوئی کہے: ’’سب یہی کر رہے ہیں۔‘‘ شامل ہونے سے پہلے خود سے پوچھیں: ’’اگر یہ فیصلہ صرف مجھے کرنا ہوتا، تو کیا میں پھر بھی اسے درست سمجھتا؟‘‘
levelUp: ان بہانوں پر نظر رکھیں: ’’صرف ایک بار۔‘‘ ’’صرف تھوڑا سا۔‘‘ ’’کسی کو پتا نہیں چلے گا۔‘‘ ’’سب متفق ہیں۔‘‘ اگر آپ خود کو ان میں سے کوئی بہانہ بناتے ہوئے سنیں تو رک جائیں اور دوبارہ غور کریں۔ دوسرے لوگ آپ کے انتخاب پر اثر ڈال سکتے ہیں۔ وہ آپ کی ذمہ داری اپنے اوپر نہیں لے سکتے۔ اگلی قسط: اصول دشمن نہیں تھا

### قسط 5 · اصول دشمن نہیں تھا
title: دیکھیں، یہ حد کس چیز کی حفاظت کر رہی ہے؟
mission: گھر کے کسی ایسے مناسب اصول کا انتخاب کریں جو کبھی کبھی آپ کو پریشان کرتا ہو۔ خود سے پوچھیں: ’’یہ اصول کس شخص، حق، ذمہ داری یا مستقبل کے نتیجے کی حفاظت کر رہا ہو سکتا ہے؟‘‘ آج کسی کے یاد دلانے کا انتظار کیے بغیر اس اصول کا احترام کرنے کی کوشش کریں۔
levelUp: جب آپ گھر کے کسی اصول سے اختلاف کریں تو تین مراحل کی مشق کریں: احترام سے پوچھیں۔ غور سے سنیں۔ ذمہ داری سے جواب دیں۔ کوئی حد اس وقت رکاوٹ محسوس ہو سکتی ہے جب ہم صرف یہ دیکھتے ہیں کہ وہ ہمیں کس چیز سے روک رہی ہے۔ سمجھ داری تب شروع ہوتی ہے جب ہم یہ بھی دیکھیں کہ وہ حد کس چیز کی حفاظت کر رہی ہے۔ حضرت صالح علیہ السلام کی کہانی حضرت صالح علیہ السلام کی پانچ اقساط کا اختتام اس سفر کے پانچ اسباق: قسط 1 — ہر چیز کے لیے اصول کیوں ہیں؟ کسی کام کو کرنے کی قدرت ہونا ہمیشہ یہ معنی نہیں رکھتا کہ ہمیں اسے کرنے کا حق بھی حاصل ہے۔ قسط 2 — لیکن مجھے بتائیں کیوں؟ احترام کے ساتھ کیے گئے سوالات سمجھ حاصل کرنے کی کوشش ہوتے ہیں، لیکن سمجھ آنے کے ساتھ ذمہ داری بھی آتی ہے۔ قسط 3 — یہ میری باری تھی۔ کسی دوسرے کا حصہ اس وقت بھی اس کا حق رہتا ہے، جب آپ اسے حاصل کرنا چاہتے ہوں۔ قسط 4 — امی کو کبھی معلوم نہیں ہوگا۔ گروہ آپ کے فیصلے پر اثر انداز ہو سکتا ہے، لیکن آپ کی ذمہ داری اپنے ذمے نہیں لے سکتا۔ قسط 5 — اصول دشمن نہیں تھا۔ ایک مناسب حد ممکن ہے کسی ایسی چیز کی حفاظت کر رہی ہو جسے آپ ابھی دیکھ نہیں سکتے۔
