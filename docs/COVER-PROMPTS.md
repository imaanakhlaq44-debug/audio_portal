# Qissora — Series Cover Prompts

One cover per series. The same cover is used for the English and the Urdu
version, so **no text goes inside the image** — the app prints the title
beside it, and AI tools misspell words (Urdu especially).

## How to deliver the files

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | Square **1:1**, at least **1024 × 1024** (larger is fine) |
| Format | PNG or JPG — I convert to WebP |
| Folder | `repo/Covers/` |
| Name | Exactly the file name given under each series below |

**Keep the important part in the middle.** The home screen crops covers to
4:3, so roughly the top and bottom 12% can be cut off. Faces and the main
object should sit in the central area.

---

## Rules for every cover

1. **Never show a Prophet (A.S.)** — no face, body, silhouette, shadow, or
   glowing figure. Prophet series use symbols only (a boat, carved rock homes,
   stars and a pen...). Imaan and Akhlaq appear as themselves in the present
   day.
2. **No angels, no Iblis/Shaytan, no depiction of Allah's light as a figure.**
3. No text, letters, logos or watermarks inside the image.
4. Modest clothing: Imaan in a long kameez with shalwar; if a dupatta or hijab
   is shown, keep it consistent across covers.
5. Children look like the brand logo — use the brand logo image as a
   **character reference** in the tool if it accepts one.

## Style block (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Warm hand-painted children's storybook illustration, soft painterly
> brushwork, gentle textured shading, rounded friendly shapes, bright but soft
> daylight, cozy and hopeful mood, South Asian Muslim family setting. Colour
> palette led by raspberry pink #CA2962, warm orange #DD7A10 and deep navy
> #192F52, with soft sky blue and cream accents. Two recurring characters:
> **Imaan**, a kind, thoughtful girl of about 10 with long brown hair in a side
> braid, big brown eyes and a pink embroidered kameez; and **Akhlaq**, her
> cheerful, playful younger brother of about 8 with messy black hair, big
> brown eyes and a teal t-shirt. Square 1:1 composition, main subject centred
> with generous margin at top and bottom, no text, no letters, no logo.

## Negative prompt (if the tool has one)

> text, letters, words, watermark, logo, signature, prophet figure, holy
> figure, halo, angel, wings, glowing person, silhouette of a man in robes,
> scary, dark, horror, realistic photo, 3D render, plastic look, extra
> fingers, distorted faces, immodest clothing

## Trailer animation — The Golden Balance

Each series opens with a short trailer: the narrator reads the series title,
the moral value and the list of episodes (about 50 seconds for Fairness). The
animation plays over that audio.

**Tool:** image-to-video (start from `fairness.png`), 5–10 second loops, then
chain clips to cover the length, or one longer generation if available.

**Clip 1 — Opening (0–10 s)**
> Starting from the Golden Balance cover image: slow gentle camera push-in
> towards the golden balance scale; morning sunlight sweeps across the
> street; bunting flutters softly; the two pans of the scale sway slightly and
> settle perfectly level with a soft golden sparkle. Imaan and Akhlaq blink
> and smile at each other. Calm, magical, storybook motion. No text.

**Clip 2 — Clues montage (10–40 s, while episode names are read)**
> Gentle storybook page-turn transitions between small painted moments: an old
> photograph with a mysterious shadow, a bronze key turning in a lock, a
> garden gate creaking open, a stone bridge path, a community hall, a card
> with the number seventeen, a narrow hidden passage lit by a lantern. Each
> moment holds for about three seconds with soft parallax motion. Warm
> palette of pink, orange and navy. No text.

**Clip 3 — Closing (40–50 s)**
> Return to the golden balance scale in the town square at sunset; Imaan and
> Akhlaq stand on either side and place their hands gently on the pedestal;
> small floating hearts in pink, orange and navy rise and sparkle; slow
> pull-back to a wide shot of Amanpur. Peaceful, hopeful ending. No text.

Series titles and episode names should be added afterwards in a video editor
(using the brand fonts *Hey Haters* and *Sassoon Infant Std*), not generated
inside the video.
